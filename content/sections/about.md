My path into software engineering began with curiosity, not a classroom. After several years selling mobile technology, I got curious about the software behind the devices — so I taught myself programming and earned a scholarship to Grand Circus in Detroit, where I launched my career in tech.

Today I'm a Software Quality Engineer focused on reliable, high-quality software through manual testing, API validation, and test automation. I've grown from Quality Assurance into Quality Engineering — automation, testing strategy, CI/CD pipelines, and DevOps practices — with an increasing focus on reliability-driven engineering. I hold an ISTQB Foundation Level certification and have completed Google Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) training.

Outside of work I mentor, volunteer, and support the Detroit tech community — and I travel to competitive fighting game events.
